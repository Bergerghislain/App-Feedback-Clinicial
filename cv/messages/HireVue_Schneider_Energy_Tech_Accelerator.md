## Q1 — Best work achievement (and why it mattered)

**Tell us what has been your best work achievement to this point in your career. What made this achievement important?**

My best achievement so far was building a research data platform at Gustave Roussy with CentraleSupélec’s MICS lab.

The challenge wasn’t just writing code. We had sensitive research data, users who weren’t developers, and unclear requirements. I owned the delivery end to end: I clarified needs with researchers, designed the PostgreSQL model, built Python/FastAPI APIs with proper authentication and access control, and delivered a React interface people could actually use. I also improved critical SQL reads by about 30% and put in place tests, CI, and documentation so the platform could live beyond me.

What made it important is the impact. It wasn’t a demo — it became a real tool that helped multidisciplinary teams trust and use their data every day. I learned how to ship under constraints, talk to non-technical stakeholders, and take ownership of quality. That’s the kind of achievement I’m proud of: something useful, reliable, and used.

I’m based in Paris, fluent in English and French, and ready for a permanent role with two intense six-month missions.
